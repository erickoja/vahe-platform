Behind Prong Studio

Built by a Jeweller, for Jewellers.

I'm Eric Kojadelian, the owner of VAHÉ Jewellery. I've spent the past seven years running the business and working directly with the public, but at heart, I'm a manufacturing jeweller with a background in trade work.

I'm hands-on across nearly every stage of production, including CAD design, 3D printing, casting, assembly and diamond setting.

I built Prong Studio to make jewellers' lives easier, particularly when it comes to quoting, organisation and managing jobs. It isn't about automating everything. It's a practical tool built by a jeweller who understands the day-to-day challenges of running a jewellery business.

— Eric Kojadelian · VAHÉ Jewellery

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Prong Studio?
A workshop platform for jewellers — it handles quoting, online proposals, production tracking, clients, repairs, stock and invoicing in one place, designed around how real bench work runs, whether you sell to the public or manufacture for the trade.
Who built it?
Eric Kojadelian — a working custom jeweller who runs VAHE Jewellery and trained as a manufacturing jeweller before that. He does the setting, CAD, printing and casting himself, and built Prong Studio for his own studio. It's shaped by real bench work, not software-company guesswork.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — every new studio starts with a 14-day free trial, no card required. Sign up, set up your studio and start quoting straight away. After the trial it's $49/month AUD — or $500/year (about $88 off) — cancel any time.
Can my clients pay by card?
Yes — add your own Stripe, PayPal or bank payment link and a "Pay online" button appears on the invoices you send. You use whatever provider you already have; nothing new to sign up for.
Does it work with my accounting software?
You can export your invoices to a CSV any time and import them into MYOB, Xero, or hand them to your bookkeeper. Direct accounting sync is on the roadmap.
Do quotes use live metal prices?
Yes — metal prices update automatically, with cast vs fabricated rates and a white-gold premium built in, so your quote reflects today's cost, not last month's.
Is my data mine?
Always. Export your invoices whenever you want — no lock-in, no exit fee. Each studio's data is isolated and private.
Who is it for?
Independent jewellers and small studios — bespoke and custom work for the public, and manufacturing jewellers who work for the trade. If you quote jobs, track them through the bench, and invoice, it's built for you.
